We will now show you a hint about how well you did on the math test. Here's how we'll do that:



We have two boxes each containing two red/or black balls. One is labeled "Pass" and the other is labeled "Fail." You will be shown the contents of the boxes. Then the boxes are hidden and shuffled such that you will not know which is which. If you answered at least {% player.passing_threshold %} questions correctly, then you will "Pass" the quiz and a ball will be randomly chosen from the "Pass" box and shown to you. Otherwise, you will "Fail" the quiz and a ball will be randomly chosen from the "Fail" box and shown to you. However, you will not know which box we will pick a ball from.

You will be shown two options for the configuration of the boxes: Option A and Option B. Please click on the buttons to see the configuration of the boxes.
